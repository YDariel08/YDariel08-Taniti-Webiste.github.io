Where can you eat?

Taniti counts with many restaurants for people to enjoy, 10 to be exact! Five of these restaurants serve mostly local fish and rice. Three other restaurants serve American-style meals. And lastly, two other restaurants serve Pan-Asian cousine! So make sure to try them all!

Grocery stores

Taniti has two supermarkets, two smaller grocery stores, and one convenience store that is open 24 hours a day.
