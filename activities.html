Dont be fooled by Taniti's small size! We have many things for tourists to enjoy when they visit the island. Most of the island activities can be located in Merriton Landing on the north side of Yellow Leaf Bay. Now, what are these activities? Here is a list of some of the things you can do while you're here:

    Things to do:
  • Visit our sandy and rocky beaches
  • Visit our rainforests
  • Zip-lining
  • Visit our local history museum!
  • Dance clubs and arcades!
  • Take a helicpoter ride
  • Go to the movies, art gallery, or bowling!
  • And many more to come!

We also have sightseeing!

Most tourists spend most of their time in Taniti City, which boasts native architecture and nearby white, sandy beaches that encircle Yellow Leaf Bay. Other popular activities include boat or bus tours of the island, hikes in the rainforest, or visits to Taniti’s active volcano.
