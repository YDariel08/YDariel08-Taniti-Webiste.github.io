    Frequently asked questions:
  1. Whats the voltage of the power outlets? - 120 volts which is the same as in the US!
  2. Drinking hours? - Alcohol is not allowed to be served or sold between 12am-9am
  3. Whats the legal drinking age in Taniti? - The drinking age in Taniti is 18, however it is not strictly enforced.
  4. Is English spoken in Taniti? - Yes! Many younger Tanitians speak fluent English. However it is harder to find people who speak English on the rural sides of Taniti.
  5. How many hospitals are in Taniti? - There is one hospital in Taniti and several clinics. The hospital has many multilingual employees.
  6. What is the crime rate? - While the crime rate in Taniti very low, the rise in tourism is causing a raise in crimes like pick pocketing and other petty crimes.
  7. Are holidays celebrated in taniti? - Yes! Taniti enjoys a large number of holidays, and many tourist attractions and restaurants will be closed on holidays, so make sure to plan accordingly!
  8. What is the Tanitian currency? - Taniti utilizes the U.S. dollar as its currency. However, euros and yen is also accepted by many businesses. Several banks offer currency exchange, and many businesses acept major creit cards too!
