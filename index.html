Welcome to Taniti!

Taniti is a small tropical island in the Pacific. It has an area of less than 500 square miles and has varied terrain! Some things you can immediately notice are it's sandy and rocky beaches, a small harbor, lush tropical rainforests, and its mountanous interior which includes a small actiuve volcano!
